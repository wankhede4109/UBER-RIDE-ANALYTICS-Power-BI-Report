# UBER-RIDE-ANALYTICS-Power-BI-Report
In this project, I transformed Uber ride data into an interactive 6-page report designed to turn raw data into meaningful business insights.

   What I worked on:

1️.  Action Buttons & Navigation
    Created interactive action buttons that allow users to navigate between dashboard pages with a single click.

2.  View 1 – Overall Performance
    Used multiple visuals to analyze booking trends and ride performance.
    Added business questions with their answers and included a dedicated summary section for quick insights.

3.  View 2 – Vehicle Type Analytics
    Analyzed different vehicle categories based on bookings, completed rides, ride distance, and ratings, along with a        summary of key findings.

4.  View 3 – Revenue Analytics
    Analyzed total booking value, average booking value, payment methods, top customers, and total ride distance.

5.  View 4 – Cancellation Intelligence
   Analyzed both customer and driver cancellations, including cancellation reasons and their contribution to overall         cancellations.

6.  View 5 – Rating & Quality
    Compared customer and driver ratings across different vehicle types and identified the highest-rated categories.

    Key Skills Used:
• Power BI
• DAX Functions
• Data Visualization
• KPI & Business Metrics
• Interactive Dashboard Design
• Data Analysis & Insights
• Business Question & Answer Analysis
